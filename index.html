How AI finds facts before it answers.

Retrieval augmented generation — “RAG” for short — lets an AI look through trusted information first, then use what it finds to write a useful answer.

Prerequisite · Why RAG?

AI needs the right facts before it answers

A language model predicts a useful response from patterns it learned earlier. It does not automatically know your private handbook or whether a policy changed yesterday, so it can confidently produce a wrong answer.

In plain English RAG lets the AI check a trusted source first. That makes the answer more current, traceable, and less likely to be invented.

RAG doesn’t give AI new memory. It gives AI a good reference.

  • 1
    RetrieveFind the small pieces of trusted information most related to the question.
  • 2
    AugmentAdd those pieces to the instructions sent to the language model.
  • 3
    GenerateWrite a clear answer using the retrieved evidence as the source of truth.
